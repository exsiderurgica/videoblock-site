01
THE IDEA

Cut first.
Arrange after.

VideoBlock avoids the dense desktop timeline metaphor. One clip stays large on screen. You scrub on the picture, use the Blade at the exact point you want, then assemble the resulting pieces in the Blocks view.

02
BLOCK EDITING

Your edit is a sequence of blocks.

ACTUAL ANDROID UI

Arrange the cut visually.

Tap a Block to cue it, long-press and drag to change the order, remove unwanted Blocks without touching the original media, then export the current sequence.

AUDIO / SYNC / EDIT

External audio is a project track.

ACTUAL ANDROID UI

Sync by eye and by ear.

The real Audio / Sync screen keeps the waveform, offset controls, normalization and playback together so external audio can stay aligned under the project.

AUTO SYNCMANUAL OFFSETWAVEFORMSYNC PLAY

MANUAL SYNC

Drag external audio against the block sequence, then fine-adjust it in milliseconds. Auto Sync is only a starting point.

TRANSIENT SNAP

Snap is optional. Leave it off for completely manual cuts, or jump to previous / next detected transients when useful.

IN / OUT + LOOP

Choose the exact beginning and end of an audio edit, loop that range and refine it while listening.

AUDIO-ONLY EDITOR

Import audio without any video, cut it into independent blocks and export the retained result as PCM WAV.

04
CAMERA / RAW AUDIO

Shoot picture and keep the audio master.

CAMERA2 CAPTURE

Rear/front camera switching, lens selection, orientation handling, clean preview and native stabilization when the active device mode supports it.

RAW WAV SIDECAR

Every completed camera take saves the final MP4 plus a separate timestamp-matched RAW WAV. Video is stored in Movies/VideoBlock and the RAW audio in Music/VideoBlock/Camera RAW.

LIVE AUDIO METERS

Select AUTO, phone mic or USB-C input and monitor L/R dBFS, waveform and clipping. At 0.0 dB input trim the recorder keeps the RAW path; non-zero trim is explicitly digital.

MANUAL + DEVICE TOOLS

Exposure, focus and white-balance controls are exposed when supported. Bluetooth monitoring and Nothing Glyph / REC-light functions remain device-dependent.
